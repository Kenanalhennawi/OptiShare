OptiShare Privacy Policy

Effective date: 29 August 2026

OptiShare is designed for direct, user-initiated transfers between nearby devices. It has no advertising, analytics, tracking SDKs, mandatory account, or developer-operated transfer cloud.

Information handled by the app

To provide transfers, OptiShare may process files and clipboard content selected by you, file names, types and sizes, nearby device names, local network addresses, device trust fingerprints, transfer status, and local transfer history. This information is used on your devices to discover a receiver, authenticate a session, transfer the selected content, resume interrupted transfers, and verify integrity.

Collection and sharing

OptiShare does not send your information to the developer, sell personal information, use it for advertising, or share it with data brokers. Content is sent only to the receiving device you choose. A transfer necessarily discloses the selected content and its required metadata to that receiving device.

Local network and security

OptiShare uses the local network and nearby-device facilities for discovery and transfer. Every transfer route exposed in the Android-only release uses authenticated, application-level encryption and integrity verification. Experimental companion compatibility code is not exposed in this release.

Permissions

Depending on your Android version and the feature you choose, OptiShare may request nearby Wi-Fi or location permission for local device discovery, notification permission for active-transfer status, access to files or media you explicitly select, and camera access only when a QR feature is used. OptiShare does not use these permissions for advertising or profiling.

Storage and retention

Received files remain on the receiving device until you delete them. Transfer history and trusted-device records are stored locally and can be removed by clearing them in the app or clearing app data. Temporary resumable-transfer data is kept locally while needed and stale private partial-transfer data may be removed automatically after 30 days.

Children

OptiShare is a general-purpose utility and is not directed specifically to children under 13. It does not knowingly collect children's personal information for the developer.

Changes

Material changes to this policy will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
