# OptiShare Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** September 8, 2026

OptiShare is a local file-sharing application designed to transfer files directly between devices. This Privacy Policy explains how OptiShare handles information when you use the Android application.

## Summary

- OptiShare does not require an account.
- OptiShare does not contain advertising or third-party analytics SDKs.
- OptiShare does not upload your files, clipboard content, or transfer history to an OptiShare cloud service.
- Nearby transfers are sent directly between devices over a local network or Wi-Fi Direct and are protected by application-level encryption.

## Information processed by the app

OptiShare processes only the information required to provide features you choose to use:

- **Files and media:** Files, photos, videos, music, documents, applications, folders, text, or clipboard content that you explicitly select for transfer.
- **Device and connection information:** A device display name, local network address, connection capabilities, and temporary discovery information used to find and connect to nearby OptiShare devices.
- **Trusted-device information:** Locally stored device identifiers, public-key fingerprints, and routing preferences used for trusted pairing and SmartRoute.
- **Transfer information:** File names, types, sizes, transfer status, timestamps, integrity hashes, resume checkpoints, and local transfer history.
- **App preferences:** Language, theme, notification, sound, vibration, visibility, and storage preferences.

OptiShare does not sell personal information and does not use transferred content for advertising, profiling, or analytics.

## How information is used

Information is processed to:

- discover nearby OptiShare devices;
- establish an encrypted device-to-device session;
- send, receive, pause, resume, and verify transfers;
- prevent corrupted or incomplete files from being finalized;
- display received files and transfer history;
- remember devices that you explicitly trust; and
- apply your local app settings.

## Network communication and security

Transfers occur directly between participating devices using the local network or Wi-Fi Direct. Transfer sessions use authenticated encryption and cryptographic integrity verification. Security codes or QR pairing may be used to confirm that you are connecting to the intended device.

A QR pairing code may contain temporary local connection information. It should be shared only with the intended recipient. OptiShare does not use QR codes to carry the transferred file itself.

## Storage and retention

Received files are stored on your device, normally under the OptiShare folders in Downloads and their applicable media categories. Preferences, trusted-device records, transfer history, and resume checkpoints are stored locally on your device.

You can remove received files and local history from within OptiShare or Android storage tools. Uninstalling the app removes its private local app data, while files saved in shared storage may remain until you delete them.

## Permissions

Depending on your Android version and the feature you use, OptiShare may request access to nearby devices, Wi-Fi state, location for legacy Wi-Fi discovery, notifications, camera for QR scanning, and selected files or media. Permissions are used only for the requested OptiShare function. You can revoke permissions through Android Settings, although the related feature may stop working.

## Sharing with other parties

OptiShare sends selected content only to the receiving device you choose. The app does not transmit your content to the developer for storage. Device manufacturers, operating-system providers, network operators, and app stores may independently process information under their own privacy policies.

## Children

OptiShare is a general-purpose utility and is not specifically directed to children. The app does not knowingly collect children's personal information through a developer-operated server.

## Your choices

You control which content is selected, which device receives it, whether a device is trusted, when your device is visible, and whether local transfer records or received files are retained. You can revoke trusted devices and app permissions at any time.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when OptiShare features or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are published.
